# OnlyEQ 1.3.5

- High and low shelves near or above 20 kHz now keep their shape. A +12 dB high shelf at 15 kHz on a 44.1 kHz device used to fall back to the cramped bilinear design; it now uses Vicanek's two-pole shelving fit and tracks the intended curve within about 0.6 dB. Shelves with non-Butterworth Q use a generalised fit and keep the older design when that fits better.
