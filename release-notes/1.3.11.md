# OnlyEQ 1.3.11

- The editor's bottom bar keeps only Preamp, Auto, and the peak meter. Crossfeed and the limiter are global settings, not part of a preset, so they live in the popover and Settings › Sound only.
- The crossfeed picker in Settings is labelled Profile, so "preset" means one thing.
- Both preset menus offer one "Delete “name”…" item for the current custom preset instead of a nested list of every preset.
