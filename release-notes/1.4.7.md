# OnlyEQ 1.4.7

Compact band list in the editor.

- Bands are rows in two columns (three in a wide window) instead of cards in a horizontally scrolling strip. A ten-band preset shows whole; longer presets scroll vertically.
- Each row holds the type menu, frequency, gain, and Q fields, and the delete glyph. The selected band's row is tinted in its colour.
- Add Band is the last row of the list.
