# OnlyEQ 1.4.4

A quieter popover.

- Bypass and Crossfeed are small toggles under the axis; the Loudness toggle is gone from the popover (it sits in Settings › Sound), since next to the volume slider the word read as volume.
- The preset's device binding is one checkbox, "Use automatically on <device>", which both makes the binding and undoes it.
- The row symbols lose their grey discs, the volume slider becomes a small control at the end of the device row (the percentage shows while you drag, and stays orange in the boost zone), and the footer is a hairline with Open Equalizer… and the gear. Import moved into the preset menu.
- Bypass now eases the curve onto the 0 dB line instead of blanking the plot.
- The screenshot renderer no longer activates the app for each render, so running it stops stealing focus.
