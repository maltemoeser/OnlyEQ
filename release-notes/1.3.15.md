# OnlyEQ 1.3.15

- Band cards take keyboard focus. Tab to a card to select its band, then use the arrow keys: up and down change gain by 0.5 dB, left and right move the frequency a semitone. Hold Option for 0.1 dB and quarter-semitone steps. Delete still removes the selected band, and every nudge is one undo step.
- VoiceOver reads each graph handle as "Band 3, Peak" with its frequency, gain, and Q, and adjusts gain with the usual swipe or arrow gestures.
- The switches in Devices and Shortcuts now have names for VoiceOver.
- The curve in the popover is a real button: it takes focus and opens the editor with Space.
- Animations are skipped when Reduce Motion is on.
- Value readouts in the editor and Settings grow with larger text sizes instead of clipping.
