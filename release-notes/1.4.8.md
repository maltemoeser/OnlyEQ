# OnlyEQ 1.4.8

Adjust pane for tuning a profile without editing its bands.

- The editor's Preamp bar gains a Bands / Adjust switch. Adjust shows Bass, Treble, Tilt, and Strength sliders over the preset's correction: Bass and Treble are ±6 dB shelves at 105 Hz and 2.5 kHz, Tilt tips the response about 1 kHz, and Strength scales every band's gain from 0 to 100 %.
- The adjustment is saved with the preset, kept apart from its bands. Compare, Bypass, Revert, Undo, and Auto preamp all include it. Presets saved by earlier versions load as neutral.
- The graph keeps its handles on the bands and draws the curve of what is heard, adjustment included; the popover does the same.
- Settings › About names the upstream project the fork is based on.
