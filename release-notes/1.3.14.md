# OnlyEQ 1.3.14

- Each band keeps its colour for life. Deleting a band no longer recolours the bands after it on the graph and in the cards, and undo puts back exactly what it removed.
- New bands take the lowest free colour, so a preset never shows two bands in the same hue.
- Colours are saved with the preset. Presets saved by earlier versions get colours in band order on first load.
