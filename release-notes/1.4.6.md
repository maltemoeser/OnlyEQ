# OnlyEQ 1.4.6

Popover and editor tidied after a third design review.

- The volume fader's track now spans the plot's full height, so the knob reaches the top; the readout sits under it on the axis line.
- The output device is a capsule pull-down beside Bypass and Crossfeed. Both toggles are text-only, so a long device name fits.
- One glyph per meaning: the preset row shows a waveform, Open Equalizer… and Import… are plain words, and Revert in the editor toolbar is the restore arrow (the uturn arrow stays Undo's). The editor's Bypassed banner shares the popover's crossed-waveform glyph.
- The editor's Compare row has room under the toolbar.
- Band cards run low to high in frequency, matching the graph left to right. The order holds still while a node is dragged.
- The popover no longer reserves empty space under the footer.
- ⌘B toggles Bypass in the editor.
- VoiceOver can adjust the volume fader in 5 % steps.
