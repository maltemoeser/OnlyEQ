# OnlyEQ 1.3.22

- The A/B control is now a Compare row under the toolbar with two named slots. Each slot shows the preset it holds, with "(edited)" when the curve differs from the saved preset. The selected slot is what you hear and edit; the other waits unchanged.
- An empty slot reads "Choose…" and opens the preset list (or Import…). Choosing fills the slot and switches to it. Nothing is saved until you press Save, and the tooltips say so.
- Choosing a preset for a slot is one undo step, named "Compare with <preset>".
